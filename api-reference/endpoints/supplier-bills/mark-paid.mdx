---
title: 'Mark Bill as Paid'
openapi: 'POST /supplier-bills/{id}/mark-paid'
description: 'Record a payment on a supplier bill. Supports partial payments.'
---

## Overview

Records a payment on the supplier bill. Pass `payment_amount` lower than the
bill total to record a partial payment (status becomes `partial`). When the
amount is omitted, the full `tax_inclusive` total is used. `payment_date`
defaults to today.

## Linked expense

If the bill is linked to a `FirmExpense`, that expense is kept in sync — its
`is_paid` flag and `paid_at` date are updated to match.
