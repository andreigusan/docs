---
title: 'Mark Bill as Unpaid'
openapi: 'POST /supplier-bills/{id}/mark-unpaid'
description: 'Reset a supplier bill to unpaid status, clearing payment fields.'
---

## Overview

Clears the bill's `payment_date` and `payment_amount`, sets `status` to
`unpaid`, and (if the bill is linked to an expense) flips that expense back
to unpaid.
